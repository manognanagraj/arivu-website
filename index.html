SECTION 01

Who We Are

Empowering Every Generation Through Knowledge & Well-being

ARIVU is an awareness-driven educational initiative committed to making mental health, emotional well-being, and life skills accessible to everyone. Through evidence-based workshops, meaningful conversations, and experiential learning, ARIVU empowers individuals to build resilience, develop self-awareness, strengthen relationships, and create emotionally healthier communities.

Rashitha Nagraj Founder, ARIVU

SECTION 02 · FOUNDER

The Vision Behind ARIVU

Leading Change Through Compassionate Education

Rashitha Nagraj is a Counselling Psychologist, Mental Health Coach, and Special Educator dedicated to making mental health education practical, accessible, and impactful. With experience working closely with children, adolescents, parents, and educators, she combines psychology, life skills, and holistic approaches to help individuals build confidence, resilience, and emotional well-being.

"Awareness is the first step toward meaningful change."

Our Belief

We believe that awareness should begin early, conversations should be safe, and education should prepare people not only for careers but for life. Emotional intelligence, empathy, resilience, and informed decision-making are essential life skills.
